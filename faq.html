FAQ & How It Works

Everything you need to know about using Subtly to add your own subtitles to any video.

What Subtly actually does

A huge amount of video content online — foreign-language films, regional TV shows, interviews, lectures, and hobbyist uploads — never gets official subtitles in your language. YouTube itself doesn't let viewers attach their own unofficial subtitle files to someone else's video, which leaves that content effectively locked away from anyone who doesn't already speak the original language.

Subtly solves this by playing the video normally (through YouTube's own official embedded player) while overlaying a subtitle file that you provide, timed to match the video as it plays. Nothing about the video itself is modified, downloaded, or redistributed — Subtly simply displays your subtitle text on top of the video at the right moments, entirely within your own browser.

Getting started

1. Find or create a subtitle file

You'll need a subtitle file in either .srt or .vtt format — the two most common subtitle formats used across the industry. These can come from a variety of places: a translation you've written yourself, a fan-subtitling community, an auto-transcription tool, or a subtitle file you've extracted from another source you legally own. Subtly doesn't generate or search for subtitle files on your behalf — you supply the file.

For videos that genuinely have no subtitles anywhere — a common problem with regional shows, older uploads, or niche content — a transcription tool like TurboScribe can generate one for you: paste the video's link there, transcribe it, and export the result as an .srt or .vtt file to bring back here. This is a separate, independent tool — not something Subtly operates — so its own free-tier limits and terms apply.

2. Paste the video link

Copy the URL of the video from YouTube (a standard youtube.com/watch?v=... or youtu.be/... link works) and paste it into the "YouTube link" field, then tap "Load video." The video will appear using YouTube's own player, with all of its normal controls.

3. Upload your subtitle file

Tap "Choose file" under the subtitles section and select your .srt or .vtt file. Subtly reads the timestamps inside the file and prepares to display each line at the right moment.

4. Press play

Once both are loaded, just play the video as normal. Subtitles track the video's real playback position automatically — pausing, rewinding, or skipping ahead all keep the subtitles in sync, the same way built-in captions would behave on any other platform.

Troubleshooting

My subtitles seem a little early or late

This usually means the subtitle file itself was originally timed for a slightly different version or cut of the video (a very common issue with subtitle files found online). Use the "−0.5s / +0.5s" offset buttons under the player to nudge the timing until it lines up.

My subtitle file didn't load anything

Double-check that your file is a genuine .srt or .vtt file with real timestamp entries — some downloaded "subtitle" files are actually incomplete exports or placeholder files from transcription tools, especially free/trial versions, and may only contain one line or a promotional watermark instead of the full transcript.

The video won't load or play

Some videos have embedding disabled by the person who uploaded them — this is a setting controlled entirely by the video's owner on YouTube's side, and no tool can override it. If a specific video won't load, try a different one to confirm whether it's a video-specific restriction.

Subtitles disappear in fullscreen

Use Subtly's own ⛶ Fullscreen button (in the top-right corner of the video), rather than any fullscreen control belonging to the video player itself. This ensures the video and the subtitle layer expand together as one.

Accounts, saving, and Pro

Do I need an account?

You need to be signed in to use Subtly, using either Google sign-in or an email and password. This is what allows the "My Videos" feature to save your videos and subtitle files to your account so you can come back to them later.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free accounts can save up to 3 videos at a time and will occasionally see ads. Pro removes ads entirely and lifts the save limit, so you can build out an unlimited library of subtitled videos.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed through Paystack. You can manage or cancel your subscription directly from the confirmation email Paystack sends after your payment, or by contacting support through Paystack's customer channels referencing your subscription.

Your data

Subtly stores the videos and subtitle files you choose to save under "My Videos" against your account, so they're private to you. Full details on what's collected and how it's used are in our Privacy Policy.
