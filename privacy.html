Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Subtly collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Subtly is a tool for adding your own subtitle files to videos, and we've tried to keep the data we handle to the minimum needed to make that work.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign up, we collect your email address (if you use email sign-in) or basic profile information provided by Google (if you use Google sign-in), via our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you choose to save: if you use the "My Videos" feature, we store the video link, subtitle file name, and (where the file is small enough) the subtitle file's text content, associated with your account.
  • Payment information: if you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed entirely by Paystack. We do not receive or store your card details — we only receive confirmation that a payment was made.

We do not access, store, or process the actual video content you watch — videos play directly from YouTube's own servers via their official embedded player, and never pass through Subtly's systems.

How we use this information

  • To let you log in and access your saved videos across sessions and devices.
  • To enforce plan limits (e.g. the 3-saved-video limit on the Free plan) and provide Pro features to subscribers.
  • To communicate with you about your account or subscription if needed.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Advertising

Subtly may display ads served by Google AdSense to Free-plan users. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and how to opt out of personalized advertising, at Google's own Advertising Partners policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Third-party services we use

  • Firebase (Google): handles account sign-in and stores your saved videos.
  • Paystack: handles Pro subscription payments.
  • YouTube (Google): provides the video playback itself, via their official embed.
  • Netlify: hosts the Subtly website files.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on their end.

Your choices

You can delete any saved video from "My Videos" at any time from within the app. If you'd like your account and all associated data fully deleted, you can contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Subtly is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required to consent to data processing in their country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the service changes. Continued use of Subtly after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, your data, or your account, you can reach us at subtlysupport@gmail.com.
