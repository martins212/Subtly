Add your own subtitles to any video

Subtly plays videos from YouTube and overlays your own .srt or .vtt subtitle file on top, perfectly synced to playback — built for foreign-language shows, interviews, and uploads that never got official subtitles in your language.

  • Paste a video link — it plays with the real YouTube player
  • Upload your subtitle file — it syncs automatically as you watch
  • Save videos to your account and pick up where you left off
